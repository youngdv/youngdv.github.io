My DNA: The Evidence-Based Pragmatist

My path into design didn't start with Figma — it started with a B.S. in Psychology and a genuine fascination with why we do what we do.

I don't believe you need a massive research budget to design well. I believe in Evidence-Based Design. Because I spent 3+ years as a Human Factors Research Scientist running eye-tracking and simulator studies for the FHWA, I don't design by "gut feeling." I design based on how the human brain actually processes information under pressure.

The "No-Budget" Strategy: When time is tight, I lean on Heuristic Evaluations, Human Factors principles, and Accessibility Audits (WAVE) to de-risk a product before a single line of code is written.

What I Specialize In

High-Stakes Systems

I design for environments most avoid: government infrastructure and safety-critical workflows. Information hierarchies that surface what matters most, when it matters most.

Why: Because a confusing interface in these sectors doesn't just frustrate — it causes errors.

Behavioral Science

Using my M.S. in HCI, I simplify "mental taxes" (cognitive load) for users under time pressure — applying established psychological principles, not instinct.

Why: Human performance is predictable. If we know the "why," we can design a better "how."

Accessibility (508)

I build WCAG 2.1 AA compliance into the DNA of a project from day one — not as a checklist at the end. Section 508, COV RAMP, WAVE audits.

Why: Inclusion is a requirement. I ensure buttons and flows work for everyone.
