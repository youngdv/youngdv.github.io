Project overview

The Maintenance Rating Program (MRP) Web platfom is a web-based interface designed to help VDOT managers prioritize maintenance work, track status, and identify blockers early. My deliverable was a mid- to high-fidelity prototype that defined information hierarchy and interaction patterns for a production web platform.

  • Role: Sole UX Designer
  • Scope: Competitive analysis, lo-fi wireframes, and mid/high-fi prototyping
  • Research: Stakeholder- and workflow-informed (limited formal UX research)

Goals

  • Give district managers a single place to triage and assign work.
  • Make status and urgency signals clear at a glance.
  • Reduce reliance on manual data pulls across systems.
  • Support coordination between field and office teams.

Key features

  • Worklist: clear states and due-soon highlights for quick triage.
  • Role-aware panels: views tailored for ops leads and coordinators.
  • Interactive map context: location and clustering to support routing decisions.
  • Notification stream: centralized updates that deep-link into the relevant record.
  • Accessible UI: WCAG-aligned contrast and spacing for long sessions.

Competitive analysis

I reviewed comparable operational dashboards to identify patterns for summarization, map + list layouts, and notification handling, adapting them to MRP’s maintenance workflows.

  • At-a-glance summaries support rapid prioritization; I applied hierarchy and visual emphasis to surface urgency.
  • Maps in context are powerful; I balanced map size with actionable tables to keep tasks front and center.
  • Notification streams reduce context switching; the design deep-links into the exact record and filter state.

Lo-fi layout exploration

Low-fidelity wireframes were used to explore layout, information hierarchy, and relationships between views before visual refinement.
