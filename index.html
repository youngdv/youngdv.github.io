How I Work

Every project starts with understanding your users and their context before I touch a design tool.

01

Research

Stakeholder interviews, task analysis, competitive audits, behavioral observation. I map what people actually do, including the workarounds and friction points that documentation misses.

02

Design

Wireframes, prototypes, design systems. Every decision traces back to a research finding. I design for the real context of use, whether that's a busy parent on a phone or a field technician under time pressure.

03

Validation

Usability testing, heuristic evaluation, iteration. I watch where people hesitate, what they misread, and what the patterns reveal about the design's assumptions, then prioritize what to fix first.

04

Accessibility

WCAG 2.1 AA standards, Section 508 for government work. I build accessibility into the architecture from day one, not bolted on at the end.

Currently: UI/UX Designer at Leidos, designing human-system interfaces for government transportation and logistics programs.

Previously: Human Factors Research Scientist at Leidos, eye-tracking systems, driving simulators, controlled behavioral studies.
