Studio88 Keys · Freelance web design

Giving a growing piano studio one place for families to find schedule, pricing and policies

McKenzie's studio was growing through word of mouth, but without a web presence, prospective families had to email or DM just to learn the basics. I designed a 7-page site around transparent pricing and founder-forward trust signals, so families arrive informed and ready to book.

Role
Web Designer (Freelance)
Client
Studio88 Keys
Industry
Music education
Tools
Figma · Framer
Deliverable
7-page marketing site
01
The problem

The interaction cost of word of mouth

McKenzie's studio was growing, but her admin load was becoming unsustainable. Parents had to start an email chain or social media DM just to learn pricing or location. That friction led to drop-offs and left McKenzie answering the same introductory questions over and over.

She needed a site that builds trust and answers FAQs on its own, so she can focus on teaching.

02
Process

Designing for the scroll

I treated each page as a scrolling narrative and front-loaded decisions in Figma before the Framer build.

Information architecture

A no-dead-ends flow that moves parents from curiosity (Home) to validation (Tuition, Gallery) to action (Contact).

Component-driven

A library of reusable navigation, footer and Closure Card components kept the Framer build visually consistent with the comps.

Mobile-first build

High-fidelity comps translated into a responsive site that stays legible on small screens.

03
Design decisions

Every decision had a reason

Visual pacing

Background colors change between sections as a cue that the reader has moved into a new topic, which helps prevent scroll fatigue.

Scannability

Type hierarchy

An expressive serif italic in heroes for personality; clean sans-serif for Tuition and Policies so busy parents can read the details.

Legibility

Trust signals

Founder-forward photography of McKenzie and her students, plus a gallery of real recitals and certificates instead of anonymous testimonials.

Trust

Closure cards

Dated "Upcoming Closures" cards answer the most common question current families ask, before they have to email.

Fewer emails

Persistent booking path

"Book a Lesson" stays in the navigation, so enrollment is always one click away however far a parent scrolls.

Conversion
04
Deliverables

FounderMcKenzie's bio, portrait and quote
GalleryRecital and studio photos

05
Impact

From admin fatigue to self-service

Professional identity

The site presents Studio88 as a modern choice in the DMV music market.

Less admin

Pricing, schedule and policies are available any time, so "how much" and "when" questions are answered before anyone emails.

Better-informed leads

Families who reach out have already seen the pricing and policies.
