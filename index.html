How I Work

Every project starts with understanding the decision environment before I touch a design tool.

01

Research

Field observation, task analysis, cognitive walkthroughs, behavioral interviews. I map what people actually do — including the workarounds and failure points that documentation misses.

02

Design

Wireframes → prototypes → design systems. Every decision traces back to a research finding. I design for the real context of use — gloved hands, glare, time pressure, competing priorities.

03

Validation

Usability testing, heuristic evaluation, iteration. I'm not just tracking task completion — I'm watching where people hesitate, what they misread, and what patterns tell us about the system's assumptions.

04

Compliance

Section 508, WCAG 2.1 AA, government standards. I integrate accessibility and regulatory requirements from the start — built into the architecture, not bolted on at the end.

Currently: UI/UX Designer at Leidos, designing human-system interfaces for government transportation and logistics programs.

Previously: Human Factors Research Scientist at Leidos — eye-tracking systems, driving simulators, controlled behavioral studies.
