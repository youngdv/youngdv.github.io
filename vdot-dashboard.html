VDOT MRP Web Platform

Operational analytics that make maintenance priorities and status easy to scan.

Industry
Government / Transportation
Client
Virginia DOT
Timeframe
In Progress
Role
User Experience Designer

Overview

The VDOT MRP Web Platform is a web-based analytics platform designed to help district managers monitor inspection backlogs, track maintenance status, and prioritize work across regions. As the sole UX designer, I created a streamlined Web Platform interface that provides real-time visibility into field operations and supports data-driven decision-making for maintenance planning.

Who is this product for?

VDOT district managers and operations staff who oversee maintenance inspection programs across Virginia. These users need to quickly assess inspection status, identify backlogs, assign work, and coordinate between field teams and office operations.

Goals

  • Provide real-time visibility into inspection data and maintenance status across districts
  • Support decision-making with clear prioritization and urgency signals
  • Enable efficient triage and work assignment through role-based views
  • Reduce manual data pulls by centralizing operational analytics
  • Facilitate coordination between field inspectors and district managers

Why is this needed?

District managers lacked a centralized view of inspection status and maintenance priorities. Data was scattered across multiple systems, requiring manual aggregation and making it difficult to identify urgent work, track progress, or coordinate efficiently across teams. VDOT needed a Web Platform solution that could surface actionable insights and support proactive maintenance planning.

What did I work on?

  • Web Platform design and layout that prioritizes operational visibility and quick scanning
  • Data visualization patterns for inspection status, backlogs, and urgency indicators
  • Filtering and search patterns to support targeted views across districts and work types
  • Role-based UI configurations for different user types and workflows
  • Responsive layout design to support both desktop and mobile access
  • Competitive analysis of comparable operational Platforms

Who'd I work with?

I collaborated with the engineering team on technical feasibility and data structure, data analysts to understand reporting requirements and key metrics, and VDOT management to validate Web Platform priorities and role-based needs. Stakeholder feedback sessions helped refine the information hierarchy and ensure the design met operational requirements.

Impact

Delivered a centralized Web Platform that provides real-time visibility into inspection data and maintenance status. The design supports data-driven decision-making with clear prioritization signals and role-based views tailored to district manager workflows. By consolidating operational analytics in one interface, the Web Platform reduced manual effort and improved coordination between field and office teams.
