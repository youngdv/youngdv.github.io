About

Optimizing Human-System Interaction for Complex Operations

I am a Human Factors professional and UI/UX Designer at Leidos focused on improving operational efficiency and data governance in government and transportation systems. With a B.S. in Psychology and an M.S. in Human-Computer Interaction, I design and evaluate Human-System Interfaces (HSI) that translate complex workflows into actionable, accessible tools.

From Research to Implementation

My approach is grounded in scientific rigor from 3+ years at Leidos, where I began as a Human Factors Research Scientist supporting federally funded research with eye-tracking and simulator environments before transitioning into UI/UX design.

I apply Task Analysis, Heuristic Evaluation, and behavioral observation to align system requirements with user mental models, operational workflow constraints, and measurable performance goals.

Modernizing Government Infrastructure

I specialize in taking fragmented legacy systems and turning them into streamlined, compliant, and accessible operational platforms. Whether acting as the sole designer on the VDOT web platform or conducting detailed Task Analysis for PCIS dashboards, my work focuses on system clarity and operator effectiveness in complex, data-heavy environments.

  • Data Governance — simplifying dense telemetry and infrastructure backlogs into intuitive decision-support interfaces
  • Cognitive Load Management — structuring information architecture to reduce operator fatigue in data-intensive workflows
  • Validation — performing Heuristic Evaluations and serving as a Behavioral Analyst during usability testing to identify performance bottlenecks
  • Accessibility & Compliance — implementing Section 508 and WCAG 2.1 standards for federal and state agency contracts

My Philosophy

I believe the most effective systems respect human constraints while maximizing technical capability. My goal is to build tools that are compliant, functional, and fundamentally human-centered.

If you want to collaborate or talk about UX for complex systems, feel free to reach out.
